# Structural review questionnaire split into Section A (pre-authorized, informational) and Section B (new findings, response required) — this format worked well.

**Tier:** 2
**Destination:** system-prompt

## Detail

The questionnaire had 4 Section A items (from Tier 1 flags already discussed with Chris) and 8 Section B items (new structural findings). Section A items said "No response needed unless you disagree." Section B items had the standard "Implement? Yes / No / Modify: ___" response lines. This let Chris shortcut the process ("Yes to all") without losing the author's paper trail. For future engagements where the operator has pre-approved items, this A/B split should be the default questionnaire format.
