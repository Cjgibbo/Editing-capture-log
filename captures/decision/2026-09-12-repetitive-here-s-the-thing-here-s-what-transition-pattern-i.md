# Repetitive "Here's the thing" / "Here's what" transition pattern identified but handled as implementation-level cleanup, not a questionnaire item.

**Tier:** 2
**Destination:** build-log

## Detail

The manuscript used "Here's the thing," "Here's what," "Here's something" as a crutch transition in nearly every chapter. This was noted during the structural read but folded into the bullet-to-prose and general cleanup work during implementation rather than surfaced as a separate questionnaire item. It didn't require an author decision — it's a mechanical writing pattern, not a structural choice. Future manuscripts with similar AI-generated transition patterns can be handled the same way: fix during implementation, note in the briefing.
