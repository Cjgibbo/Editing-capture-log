# Diagram placeholders retained in clean files — artwork production handled outside the editing pipeline.

**Tier:** 2
**Destination:** build-log

## Detail

Chris asked if diagrams could be created within the system. Agreed approach: simple diagrams (track layouts, comparison charts) can be built directly as graphics; illustrated scenes get image generation prompts. Placeholders stay in the .docx files as centered italic gray text until art is provided. Art production is a separate workstream from the editing tiers. Tier 3 cannot format around diagrams that don't exist — this is a blocking dependency for Tier 3 if the author wants them in the final book.
