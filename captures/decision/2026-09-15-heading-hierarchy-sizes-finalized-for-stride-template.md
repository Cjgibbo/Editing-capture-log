# Heading hierarchy sizes finalized for Stride template.

**Tier:** 3
**Destination:** style-reference

## Detail

•	Chapter number: Montserrat Light 13pt ("CHAPTER ONE")

	•	Chapter title: Montserrat Bold 24pt/28pt leading

	•	Section heads (Part lines): Montserrat SemiBold 14pt, 28pt space above, 10pt below

	•	Sub-heads: Crimson Pro Bold Italic 11pt, 18pt space above, 8pt below

	•	keepNext on all headings

In chapters without Part structure, all detected headings use the section-head style (Montserrat SemiBold 14pt).
