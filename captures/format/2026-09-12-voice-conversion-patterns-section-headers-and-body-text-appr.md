# Voice conversion patterns — section headers and body text approaches that worked.

**Tier:** 2
**Destination:** build-log

## Detail

Section header conversions used in this thread:

	•	"The Example for Gage" → "What This Means for Me"

	•	"The Ayrton Senna Workout (If Gage Wants to Try It)" → "The Ayrton Senna Workout (My Version)"

	•	"The Lesson for Gage" → "What I Take from This"

	•	"What Gage Can Learn" → "What I Can Learn"

	•	"For Gage — Lessons on Dealing with Unfair Systems" → "What I Take from This"

	•	"What Gage Should Remember" → "What I Want to Remember"

	•	"For Someone Like Me" (already first-person) → "Why I Wrote This"

	•	"This book exists because Gage" → "This book exists because I"

Body text pattern: "Whatever you do in life" (second-person general address) converts naturally to "Whatever I do in life" (first-person self-address). The book's voice alternates between "Senna did X" (biography) and "what I learn from this" (personal reflection) — this is natural for a young fan writing about his hero. Direct "you" addressing the reader can stay when it reads as self-talk.
