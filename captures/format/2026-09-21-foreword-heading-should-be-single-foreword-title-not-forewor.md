# Foreword heading should be single "Foreword" title — not "FOREWORD" label + "Foreword" title stacked.

**Tier:** 3
**Destination:** style-reference

## Detail

Initial build had the chapter opener pattern (label line + title line) applied to the foreword: "FOREWORD" in Montserrat Light 13pt above "Foreword" in Montserrat Bold 24pt. This looked redundant — both lines say the same thing. Fixed to single "Foreword" title in Montserrat Bold 24pt only.

Chapter openers remain two-line: "CHAPTER [WORD]" label + title. The pattern only applies when the label and title carry different information.
