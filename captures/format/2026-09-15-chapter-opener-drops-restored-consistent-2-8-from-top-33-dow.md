# Chapter opener drops restored — consistent ~2.8" from top (~33% down page), not flush to top.

**Tier:** 3
**Destination:** build-log

## Detail

Chris initially asked to eliminate chapter drops to prevent thin last pages. After seeing chapters flush to the top, he clarified: "I thought all of the chapters beginnings were gonna start about 40% down the page... just a little higher than half the page."
