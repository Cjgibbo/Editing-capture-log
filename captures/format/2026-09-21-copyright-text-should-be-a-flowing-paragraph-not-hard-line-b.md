# Copyright text should be a flowing paragraph, not hard line breaks per clause.

**Tier:** 3
**Destination:** style-reference

## Detail

Initial build had the "All rights reserved..." text split into individual paragraph lines per clause (one paragraph per line break). This caused bad wrapping on the 5.5×8.5 page — orphaned words like "the" and "brief" on their own lines. Fixed to a single flowing paragraph that wraps naturally.
