# AI-generated manuscript artifacts persist beyond Tier 1 — structural patterns (third-person framing, outline labels, bulleted lists as prose) are systemic, not isolated.

**Tier:** 2
**Destination:** build-log

## Detail

The manuscript shows strong evidence of AI-assisted drafting. Tier 1 caught and removed explicit chatbot artifacts (follow-up lists, self-correction notes). But Tier 2 revealed that AI drafting patterns are woven into the manuscript's structure: the "Opening —" labels in every chapter are prompt scaffolding left in place; the third-person "Gage" framing is a prompt artifact (the AI was told to write "for Gage" and never converted to first person); the heavy bullet-list formatting mirrors AI output style; the repetitive chapter ending pattern ("That's what makes...") is a template the AI repeated. Lesson for future engagements: when a manuscript shows AI artifacts in Tier 1, expect structural AI patterns in Tier 2. Flag this at intake or in the Tier 1 handoff so Tier 2 scope is realistic.
