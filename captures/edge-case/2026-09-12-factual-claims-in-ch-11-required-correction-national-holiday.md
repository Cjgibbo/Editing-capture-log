# Factual claims in Ch 11 required correction: "national holiday" and "currency."

**Tier:** 2
**Destination:** build-log

## Detail

Two factual issues in Ch 11's "In Brazil" section:

	1.	"A national holiday on May 1 (the anniversary of his death)" — May 1 is International Workers' Day / Labour Day in Brazil, already a national holiday. São Paulo state observes it as Ayrton Senna Day, but it's not a national holiday for Senna. Corrected to "São Paulo state observes Ayrton Senna Day on May 1."

	2.	"His face on stamps and currency" — Senna has appeared on Brazilian commemorative stamps (confirmed). Currency appearance could not be confirmed. Adjusted to "commemorative stamps" only.

These were corrected silently per Tier 1 rules (fix what's wrong) and noted in the briefing. Both flagged to Chris at delivery since they involved removing content rather than just fixing wording.
