# Suzuka 1988 narrative in Ch 6 described events from 1989 — required full section restructure, not just a date fix.

**Tier:** 2
**Destination:** build-log

## Detail

The Tier 1 handoff noted "the Chapter 6 Suzuka 1988 race narrative describes events that actually occurred in 1989." On closer read during Tier 2, the narrative structure — "Senna wins the race but Prost wins the championship on points" — is what happened conceptually in 1989 (via disqualification), not 1988. In 1988, Senna won the race AND the championship. Simply changing "Prost won" to "Senna won" wasn't enough; the entire dramatic arc of the Suzuka 1988 section was built around the wrong outcome. Resolution: removed the Suzuka 1988 section and restructured Ch 6 so the 1989 collision stands as the primary Suzuka confrontation, with the 1988 championship resolved through the season narrative and points system explanation.
