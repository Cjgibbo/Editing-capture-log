# Content repetition across chapters was the dominant structural issue — five separate instances of significant duplicate content.

**Tier:** 2
**Destination:** build-log

## Detail

The manuscript had five distinct repetition problems: (1) Monaco 1984 told in full in Ch 3 and Ch 5, (2) Donington 1993 told in full in Ch 3 and Ch 5, (3) physical demands covered in Ch 4 and Ch 7, (4) "The Cost" section repeated in Ch 4 and Ch 7 (Ch 7 explicitly said "We talked about this in Chapter 4, but it's worth repeating"), (5) Instituto Ayrton Senna covered nearly identically in Ch 10 and Ch 11. Resolution pattern for all five: assign one chapter as the owner of each topic, trim the other to a brief mention or forward/backward reference. This pattern — "one owner, one reference" — should be standard guidance for Tier 2 repetition findings.
