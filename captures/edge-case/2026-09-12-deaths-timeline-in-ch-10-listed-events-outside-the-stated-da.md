# Deaths timeline in Ch 10 listed events outside the stated date range.

**Tier:** 2
**Destination:** build-log

## Detail

Ch 10 had a section headed "Deaths during Senna's career (1984-1994):" that listed Gilles Villeneuve and Riccardo Paletti, both killed in 1982 — two years before Senna's F1 debut. The framing was factually wrong. Reframed to "deaths in and around Senna's era" so the 1982 entries sit accurately. Elio de Angelis (1986, Senna's former Lotus teammate) was noted as such for context. This is a category of error where the author's section header contradicts the content beneath it — worth watching for in future manuscripts.
