# Do not generate individual chapter PDFs — build the complete book directly.

**Tier:** general
**Destination:** system-prompt

## Detail

Chris flagged that generating and presenting individual per-chapter PDFs was a waste of tokens. The correct workflow: (1) build one proof chapter to verify the template, (2) confirm font/style decisions, (3) go straight to full book assembly. Individual chapter files are only useful for the first proof iteration. All subsequent builds should produce the assembled book PDF only. Chris's language: "There was no reason to generate and present output files as individual PDFs."
