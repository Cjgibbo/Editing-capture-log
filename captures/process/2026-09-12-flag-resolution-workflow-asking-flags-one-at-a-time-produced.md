# Flag resolution workflow — asking flags one at a time produced cleaner, faster decisions than presenting all at once.

**Tier:** 2
**Destination:** build-log

## Detail

Chris requested "Ask me one at a time" when presented with five outstanding Tier 1 flags. Walking through flags individually prevented decision fatigue and let each flag get a clear yes/no/modify answer without cross-contamination. The author is young (Gage is Chris's son), so Chris was making decisions on the author's behalf for most items. One-at-a-time also let Chris surface new needs mid-flow (e.g., asking for title suggestions during Flag 5, asking about diagram creation during Flag 4).
