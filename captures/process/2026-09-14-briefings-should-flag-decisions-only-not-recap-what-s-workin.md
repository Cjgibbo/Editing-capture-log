# Briefings should flag decisions only — not recap what's working.

**Tier:** general
**Destination:** system-prompt

## Detail

Chris's language: "Lengthy brief is unnecessary unless it's about things that need to be decided on." After delivering output, only surface items that require a decision. Do not list what's working correctly, what was verified, or what the output contains unless it's a decision point. This applies to all tier delivery briefings.
